ERECT 3D · ARCHITECTURAL RECONSTRUCTION WORKSPACE

Turn floor plans into editable 3D buildings

Import a floor plan image or PDF, or design a plan yourself. Erect 3D lets you refine walls, doors, windows, rooms, slabs, stairs, columns, roofs and materials, then inspect the building in 3D.

Design across multiple levels

Create and align floors, edit each level independently, connect them with stairs, and inspect the complete building with section and exploded views.

Walk through your model

Explore the building in first person and move between levels. Projects are saved locally in your browser.
